Frequently asked questions

Have a question?

Here you'll find answers to the questions we most often get from new customers.

What does it cost to get a website?

All our packages include a one-time setup fee of DKK 8,000. After that, you pay a monthly fee starting from DKK 500/mo for the Basic package. We're transparent about prices – there are no hidden fees, and you don't pay the setup fee until you've approved your website.

What is a Google Business Profile, and why does it matter?

Google Business Profile is your company's profile on Google – it's the box with opening hours, photos, reviews and contact info that appears when people search for your business. A well-maintained profile is essential for local visibility: customers searching for 'hairdresser in Copenhagen' or 'restaurant near me' see your profile before they even visit your website. We set up and manage the profile for you.

How quickly can my website go live?

From our first conversation to your site going live typically takes just a few days – never more than a week. We work quickly and show you the site for approval along the way. If you want changes, we make them immediately. No payment until you're satisfied.

What if I want changes to the site?

Just contact us – call or write. Changes like new images, updated opening hours or new text are handled quickly. In the Full Digital Presence package, ongoing updates are included in the monthly price. In Basic and Visibility, we bill by usage for larger changes.

What is SEO and what does it do for my business?

SEO (search engine optimisation) is the work of getting your website to rank higher in Google's search results – organically, without paid advertising. It's about technically correct construction, relevant keywords, fast loading and good user experience. The result is that more potential customers find you when they search for what you offer in Copenhagen.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes. Our agreements have no lock-in – you can cancel with one month's notice. If you stop, we ensure you receive all materials for your website, so you can continue with another provider. We believe that good results are the best reason to stay.
